Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-05. Draft, pending owner review.

Short version: we collect what we need to run your account and your learning progress, we do not sell your data, and there is no advertising or tracking in the Octopus Education apps.

Who we are

Octopus Technology is the seller and the controller of the data described here. Contact: support@octopustechnology.net. Postal address: [OWNER TO FILL: registered business address].

What we collect

Payment. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe shares with us a customer ID, your email address, and the status of your subscription.

Account. Your username, email address and sign-in details, held by our own authentication service. Passwords are stored hashed.

Learning data. Your progress, flashcard state, answers, saved structures and designs, and anything you upload (for example a sketch or photo of an answer, or a PDF you add), kept against your account.

Where it is kept

Account and learning data are stored on our own servers, not with an advertising or analytics company.

Third parties that see some data

Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy.

AI model providers. Some tutor features send your question (and an image, if you attach one) to an AI model provider to produce a reply. Features that use stored lessons and exercise banks do not.

PubChem. The Science app looks up compounds you search for on PubChem, a public database, so the search text is sent there.

Our hosting and network providers handle your requests to reach our servers and may log IP addresses.

Ads, cookies and tracking

The Octopus Education apps contain no advertising and no analytics or tracking scripts. They use a sign-in cookie so you stay logged in, and your browser may store your settings and progress locally.

This marketing site (octopustechnology.net) loads no advertising scripts, but it does publish an ads.txt file that authorises an ad seller for our separate blog, and it shows an embedded third-party widget (monero.how) in its menu, which that provider can see you load. Our blog is a separate site that may show ads.

How long we keep it

We keep account and learning data while your account exists. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time (see below).
