Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026.

Short version: we collect what we need to run your account and your learning progress, we do not sell your data, and there is no advertising or tracking in the Octopus Education apps.

Who we are

Octopus Technology LLC, a Connecticut limited liability company, runs these services and is the controller of the data described here. Contact: support@octopustechnology.net.

What we collect

Payment. Payments are processed by Stripe. Card numbers go to Stripe and never reach our servers. Stripe shares with us a customer ID, your email address, and the status of your subscription.

Account. Your username, email address and sign-in details, held by our own authentication service. Passwords are stored as one-way bcrypt hashes, not as readable text. Our servers' disks are not currently encrypted at rest, so we do not claim encryption at rest.

Learning data. Your progress, flashcard state, answers, saved structures and designs, and anything you upload (for example a sketch or photo of an answer, or a PDF you add), kept against your account.

Technical data. Server and network logs, including IP addresses and request details, kept for security and troubleshooting.

Health and fitness data

If you use our health or workout-logging app, it stores what you enter: for example workouts, exercises, sets, body weight, meals and notes. We use it only to show it back to you and to run features you ask for. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for marketing.

You choose whether to enter it, and you can stop at any time. To withdraw consent and have it deleted, use the deletion procedure below. Some assistant features may send your workout history or your question to an AI model provider (see below) so that it can answer; if you do not want that, do not use the assistant with health entries. Our service is a personal logging tool. It is not a medical product, is not a substitute for professional medical advice, and is not a HIPAA-covered service. If you live in a state with a consumer health data law (for example Washington or Nevada), you have the rights described below for this data too, including access, deletion and withdrawing consent, and we do not sell it.

Where it is kept

Account and learning data are stored on servers we rent from Hetzner, not with an advertising or analytics company. The servers are in Germany. If you are outside the EU, this means your data is stored abroad; if you are in the EU, it is stored in the EU but controlled by a US company.

Third parties that see some data

Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy.

Cloudflare provides DNS, content delivery and TLS for our sites, so your requests pass through its network and it may log IP addresses and request details.

Hetzner hosts the servers that hold your data.

AI model providers. Some tutor and assistant features send your question (and an image, if you attach one) to a model provider to produce a reply. Depending on the feature, that may be Anthropic, OpenRouter (which routes to other model makers), Google (Gemini), xAI, or a model running on our own hardware. Each handles the data under its own terms. Features that use stored lessons and exercise banks do not use these.

PubChem. The Science app looks up compounds you search for on PubChem, a public database, so the search text is sent there.

Embedded widget (monero.how). Every page of this marketing site, in the Menu, embeds a widget from monero.how. Loading it lets that provider see your IP address and browser details. It is not used by the Octopus Education apps.

Others we use for operations may include Discord and Telegram (internal alerts and bots), Tailscale (private network for our administrators), GitHub (source code), and Let’s Encrypt (certificates). They are not intended to receive your account content. Migadu hosts our email, so messages you send us (and our replies) pass through it.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Ads, cookies and tracking

The Octopus Education apps contain no advertising and no analytics or tracking scripts. They use a sign-in cookie so you stay logged in, and your browser may store your settings and progress locally.

This marketing site (octopustechnology.net) loads no advertising scripts. Each page's head carries an ownership meta tag so an ad network can confirm this domain; that tag does not load an ad. The site also publishes an ads.txt file that authorises an ad seller for our separate blog. Our blog (blog.octopustechnology.net) loads Google AdSense to show ads. Google may use cookies and similar technology to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your interests; you can control that at adssettings.google.com, and Google's use is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. Some blog links are Amazon affiliate links: if you buy through one we may earn a commission, at no cost to you.

How long we keep it

We keep account, learning and health-log data while your account exists. Server logs are kept for a limited period, which we aim to hold to 90 days; conversations with the assistant to 12 months. Automatic deletion on that schedule is still being put in place. Payment records are kept as long as tax and accounting law requires. After you ask for deletion, copies in backups may persist for up to six months until they rotate out, and copies held by providers above follow their own retention.

Deleting your data

Self-service export and deletion is not built yet. To request it, email support@octopustechnology.net from the address on your account. We will confirm your identity, delete your account and its data from our systems, and tell you when it is done, within 30 days. On request we will also send you a copy of your data in a common format.

Your rights, including state residents

Depending on where you live (for example under California and other US state privacy laws, or the GDPR), you may have the right to know what we hold about you, access and correct it, receive a portable copy, delete it, and opt out of sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined, so there is nothing to opt out of. We will not treat you worse for exercising a right. Email support@octopustechnology.net to make a request; we answer within 30 days. If we refuse, you may ask us to reconsider, and you may contact your state attorney general (in Connecticut, the Office of the Attorney General) or, in the EU or UK, your local data-protection authority.

Security incidents

We use access controls and keep software patched, but no system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information we will notify affected users and regulators where the law requires it, by email, without unreasonable delay.

Children

You must be at least 13 to create an account, and you confirm your age when you sign up. If you are 13 to 17, a parent or guardian must agree to our terms for you, and a parent or guardian should make any purchase. Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we will delete it and its data. If you believe we hold information from a child under 13, email support@octopustechnology.net and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change; the date above shows the latest version.
