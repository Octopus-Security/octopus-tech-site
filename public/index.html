Welcome to Octopus Technology

Octopus Technology is a small group building and running its own software. Close to thirty services live on hardware we administer ourselves, all behind a single sign-in we wrote. It started as a Discord bot and grew from there.

Most of it exists because we wanted the thing and would rather build it than rent it: a music workstation that runs in a browser, grocery pricing that compares retailers, and an assistant with real tools instead of a chat box. This site is where we write about how it works, and about what went wrong along the way.

Projects

The projects page describes what each service does and the hard part of building it. Its facts are generated from the repositories, so it does not quietly go stale.

Writing

The writing page holds our post-mortems: a startup check meant to prevent an outage that caused one, and being locked out of our own sign-in service after five mistyped passwords. Each one covers what we assumed, what failed, and what we changed.

Octopus Education

Octopus Education is one subscription covering four learning apps: a maths tutor, a coding course, interactive chemistry, and a breadboard planner for electronics. The lessons and exercise banks do not depend on an AI model.
