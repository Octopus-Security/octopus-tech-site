Octopus Education

Self-paced study material in two places: tech tracks in the Code app, and non-tech courses in the School app. Both are in development.

Where it lives

💻 Tech tracks

code.octopustechnology.net

  • 25 tracks: 17 programming languages (including Python, JavaScript, C, Rust, SQL and Bash) and 8 concept tracks
  • 23 tracks are ready; the 2 CompTIA A+ tracks are still stubs
  • Concept tracks cover computing basics, Linux, networking, security principles and OSCP-style foundations
  • Lessons plus an exercise bank; nothing runs in your browser

🎓 Non-tech courses

School: school.octopustechnology.net (launching soon)

  • The home for non-tech education: Math and Science are moving under it, along with traditional subjects and non-tech certifications
  • 1 course so far: CPT foundations, personal-trainer exam prep, still coming soon
  • Math and Science still work at their current addresses: math and science

Some tutor features use an AI model, and AI can be wrong; the lessons and exercise banks do not depend on one. Study material is not a guarantee of passing any exam. See the privacy page.
