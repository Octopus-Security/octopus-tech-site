What is in it

📐 Math

A maths tutor.

  • Lessons with a plain-English “why it matters”, the logic, and a worked example
  • A built-in exercise bank you can answer by typing, on a sketch pad or by photo
  • Cheat sheets and revision index cards
  • Maths rendered with LaTeX
  • Calculus I, all sixteen chapters, plus Precalculus and more

💻 Code

Learn to code. It is not an IDE: nothing runs in your browser.

  • Lessons in about 16 tracks, from Python, JavaScript, C and Rust to SQL, Bash and Regex
  • An exercise bank: type an answer or work it by hand
  • Puzzles to stay sharp
  • Term flashcards with spaced review
  • Your progress follows your account across devices

⚗️ Science

Interactive chemistry.

  • A colour-coded periodic table of all 118 elements
  • Bond builder: Lewis structures, shapes and polarity
  • Calculator with the working shown: molar mass, moles, gas law, titration
  • Practice drills on naming, reactions and the mole, marked with the working
  • Lessons that link straight to the matching drills

🔌 Electronics

A breadboard planner for real hardware.

  • A virtual breadboard that models real tie-point strips and refuses placements that cannot exist
  • A parts catalogue with the gotchas for each part
  • Design review that flags wiring mistakes before you solder
  • Generated firmware from your own pin assignments
  • Published reference builds to start from

Some tutor features use an AI model, and AI can be wrong; the lessons and exercise banks do not depend on one. See the privacy page.
