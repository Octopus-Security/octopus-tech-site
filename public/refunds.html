Refund Policy

Last updated 2026-10-05. Draft, pending owner review.

Plain and simple. This applies to Octopus Learn subscriptions.

Within 7 days

If you ask within 7 days of your first payment, we will refund it in full. No reason needed.

After 7 days

After that we do not give partial-period refunds. If you cancel, your access continues to the end of the period you already paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Renewals

Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Renewal payments are not refundable under this policy, except where the law requires it. If you were charged by mistake, contact us and we will look into it.

How to ask

Email support@octopustechnology.net from the address you paid with. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe and can take several days to appear.
